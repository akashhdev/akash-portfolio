## Capture notes

- Export WebP in sRGB at quality 78–85; retain an archival original separately.
- Use a locked camera for every comparison sequence.
- Do not publish internal addresses, credentials, source paths, exact sensitive routes, copyrighted paper figures, or unapproved teammate imagery.
- Send images using the filenames above; the typed manifest lives in `content/media.ts`.
